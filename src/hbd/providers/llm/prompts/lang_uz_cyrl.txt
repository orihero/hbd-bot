Write in Uzbek, Cyrillic script (ўзбекча, кирилл ёзуви). Every line of lyric, every spoken script
and every free-text field is Uzbek in Cyrillic. Do not mix scripts within a line, and do not fall
back to Latin Uzbek or to Russian.

Orthography:
- Use the Uzbek Cyrillic alphabet, including ў, қ, ғ and ҳ. Туғилган кун, қўшиқ, ўтса.
- Do not substitute the Russian у for ў, к for қ, г for ғ or х for ҳ. They are different letters
  and the difference is audible.

Pronunciation and stress:
- Uzbek stress falls on the FINAL syllable of a word. Phrase lines so the last syllable of the name
  lands on a strong beat.
- Never signal stress with capital letters. A music model reads capitals as volume, not emphasis.

Register: warm, direct, unforced. Wishes for health, long life, family and light. Avoid heavy
literary vocabulary unless the genre is shashmaqom or folk.
