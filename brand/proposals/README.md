# Bayram — six logo proposals

Six directions for the renamed bot (`@bayram_uzbot`), all built on the constants in
`../README.md`: bar width 9 fully rounded, rhythm 3:5:4, flame 2r wide by 3.889r tall,
one spot of magenta per mark and it is always the flame. No fonts, including in the
wordmark. Everything is generated by `generate.py` — change a constant there and all
eleven files stay consistent with each other.

| # | File | What it is | Where it wins | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `mark-01-monogram.svg` | **Bayram B** — the initial drawn at the meter's bar width, flame on the stem | Favicon, app icon, anywhere the name is already on screen | Says the name, not the product |
| 02 | `mark-02-suzani.svg` | **Suzani rosette** — 8-fold Bukhara rosette, petals running 5:3:4:3, magenta core | Cover art, stickers, print, 32 px and up | Becomes an asterisk below 24 px |
| 03 | `mark-03-doira.svg` | **Doira** — the frame drum, 12 rim beads for the 12-count usul, 3 lit candles on the head | Everywhere. The most robust of the six | A ring is a common container |
| 04 | `04-wordmark-bayram.svg` | **BAYRAM** — stroke 12, round caps, AY/YR/AM kerned by hand | Headers, packaging, the `/start` card | Do not lock it to 01 — two flames fight |
| 05 | `mark-05-bubble.svg` | **Message bubble** — a Telegram message with the song still burning inside | Store listings, ads, onboarding | Most elements; bars merge below 24 px |
| 06 | `mark-06-ikat.svg` | **Ikat flame** — the flame rebuilt from 8 stacked bars, abr-bandi's feathered edge | Small sizes, single-colour print | All magenta, no ink; must invert on a magenta ground |

`avatar-<nn>-<name>.svg` is the BotFather cut of each mark: 512 × 512, white out of a
`#BD32AF` disc, same 9-unit clear space as `../avatar-telegram.svg`.

Recommendation: **03 Doira** as the mark, **04 BAYRAM** as the wordmark, never locked
together; **06 Ikat flame** as the sub-24 px cut. Hold **02 Suzani** for cover art.

## Regenerating

```sh
python3 brand/proposals/generate.py
```

PNG and JPEG cuts use the headless-Chrome and `sips` commands in `../README.md`.
