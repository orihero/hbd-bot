# source_motion_stills — NOT composition references for the rebuild

These eleven PNGs (`shot_01.png` … `shot_11.png`, plus the `shot_02_centreband.png` crop) are frames
lifted from the ORIGINAL SOURCE MUSIC VIDEO that the 43-second reel was derived from. They are **not**
frames of our master, `marketing/campaigns/peshta/final_polat_peshta_reel.mp4`, and they do not show our film: the
wardrobe in them is a green military tunic with gold epaulettes and a pearl head-veil, whereas the master
puts M1 in a dark suit (and, in the desert block, a navy overcoat and a wide-brim hat) and F1 in black
with gold chains, a cream ribbed turtleneck, pearl ropes or an ivory dress. Several of them are also
mid-transition composites rather than clean single setups — `shot_02.png` is a triptych with an inset
strip above row 288 and below row 1631, which is why `shot_02_centreband.png` (crop `1080x1343+0+289`)
exists beside it. They are kept only as a record of the source motion this piece was choreographed
against. **Anyone using these as composition or first-frame references will rebuild the wrong film — use
`marketing/campaigns/peshta/06_local/a_keyframes/` instead**, which is one still per sub-shot taken from the master
itself, indexed in `a_keyframes/INDEX.json`.
