### 3.2 Mobile Audio Mastering & Smartphone Speaker EQ Carve

Smartphone speakers have tiny transducers (typically 10mm to 15mm dynamic drivers) that distort severely when fed excessive sub-bass frequencies or cluttered lower-mid energy. The audio master must adhere to strict mobile engineering standards:

```
  +6 dB ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                                                        Presence Shelf
                                                        +2.0 dB (3.0 - 5.0 kHz)
   0 dB ────\──────────────────────────/─────────────\──────────────────────\────────
             \                        /               \                      \
  -3 dB ──────\                      /  Low-Mid Dip    \                      \
               \ High-Pass Filter   /   -2.5 dB         \                      \
  -6 dB ────────\ <35 Hz (24dB/oct)/   (250 - 400 Hz)   \                      \
        ─────────\────────────────/──────────────────────\──────────────────────\────
                 35 Hz          250 Hz       500 Hz      3 kHz       5 kHz     16 kHz
```

#### Detailed Mastering Parameters:
1. **Loudness Standards**:
   - **Integrated Loudness**: **-14.0 LUFS (±0.5 LUFS)**.
   - **Maximum True Peak**: **-1.0 dBFS** (strictly enforced using an oversampling brickwall limiter).
   - *Rationale*: Uploading audio louder than -14 LUFS causes Instagram and TikTok's automated transcoding servers to engage aggressive lossy compression and inter-sample peak clipping, resulting in harsh distortion.
2. **Frequency Curve (EQ Carve)**:
   - **High-Pass Filter (HPF)**: Steep 24 dB/octave cut below **35 Hz**. Frequencies under 35 Hz cannot be reproduced by mobile phone grilles and needlessly consume dynamic headroom.
   - **Low-Mid Pocketing**: Wide bell curve filter dipping **250 Hz to 400 Hz by -2.5 dB (Q = 1.2)** on the backing instrumental stem. This cleans out the "mud" and carves an acoustic pocket for vocal fundamentals.
   - **Speech Intelligibility & Presence Boost**: High-shelf boost of **+2.0 dB between 3.0 kHz and 5.0 kHz**. This ensures lyrics, consonant plosives (`p`, `t`, `k`), and sibilants (`sh`, `s`) cut through with crystalline clarity even at low phone volume.
3. **Dynamic Sidechain Ducking**:
   - The backing instrumental music track is ducked automatically by **-3.5 dB** whenever the lead parody vocals or the Telegram push chime fire.
   - **Attack Time**: 10 ms (fast enough to prevent masking, smooth enough to avoid clicks).
   - **Release Time**: 120 ms (natural musical return to full volume).
