## 1. The Instagram Reels Link Reality & Conversion Architecture

### Can you put a clickable link directly on Instagram Reels?
* **In Reels Captions (Descriptions):** **NO.** Instagram captions never render active hyperlinks. Any URL (e.g. `https://t.me/bayram_uzbot`) appears as unclickable plain text. Mobile users cannot tap or easily copy it.
* **On-Screen Link Sticker:** **NO.** Organic Reels do not have the native interactive Link Sticker found in Stories.
* **Sponsored Ads (Meta Ads Manager):** **YES.** Running the Reel as a paid boosted ad unlocks a direct clickable CTA button (*"Send Message"*, *"Learn More"*, or *"Use App"*).

### The 3 High-Conversion Workarounds for Organic Reels

```
[ Reel Impression (Hook) ]
          │
          ├──> Option 1: Comment Automation ("TABRIK" -> ManyChat -> Instant DM with Link) [HIGHEST CTR]
          ├──> Option 2: Profile Bio Link ("Havola profilda 👆" -> Taplink / t.me/bayram_uzbot)
          └──> Option 3: Immediate Story Repost with official Link Sticker [1-TAP REDIRECT]
```

1. **Comment Automation (ManyChat / Instagram Graph API):**
   * *Mechanism:* Instruct viewers in the caption and on-screen to comment a keyword (e.g., `TABRIK` or `BOT`).
   * *Why it works:* Generates explosive comment activity that boosts algorithmic distribution on the Explore and Reels tabs, while automatically delivering a 1-click clickable button to the user's DMs.
2. **"Link in Bio" (Profil shapkasidagi havola):**
   * Place `https://t.me/bayram_uzbot` in the Instagram profile website field.
   * Direct viewers in the caption: *"Havola profilda 👆"*.
3. **Story Repost with Link Sticker:**
   * Immediately upon publishing the Reel, repost it to your Instagram Story.
   * Add the native **Link Sticker** pointing directly to `https://t.me/bayram_uzbot` with sticker text: `🎁 Botga kirish (Bepul)`.
