## 4. Viral Mechanics & Execution Sequence

1. **Acoustic Dopamine**:
   The steady 96 BPM wooden table stomp and handclap captures attention in under 1 second.
2. **Duet Virality (UGC Choir Chain)**:
   The video is engineered for TikTok Duets and Reels Remixes:
   - Solo verse $\rightarrow$ 2-way split (bass) $\rightarrow$ 4-way split (harmonies & doira) $\rightarrow$ Full choir drop into chorus.
3. **The Plosive Shout "(HUH!)"**:
   At the end of line 4 of every verse, the sharp collective shout **"(HUH!)"** triggers algorithm retention and jump cuts.
4. **Call to Action**:
   Direct link to Telegram `@bayram_uzbot` with fixed 15 000 UZS pricing.
