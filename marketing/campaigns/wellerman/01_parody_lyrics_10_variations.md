## 1. Metric Structure & Singing Guide

The "Wellerman" rhythm is based on a driving, syncopated sea shanty stride. Every verse consists of 4 lines with a trochaic-iambic lilt, culminating in a rhythmic pause and a sharp, room-reverb shout **"(HUH!)"** before dropping straight into the chorus.

### Cadence Mapping:

```
[Verse Pattern]
Line 1 (~8 syllables):  Ta - ta  TA  ta - ta  TA  ta - ta
Line 2 (~10 syllables): Ta - ta  TA - ta  ta - ta  TA - ta  ta - ta
Line 3 (~8 syllables):  Ta - ta  TA  ta  TA  ta - ta
Line 4 (~7 syllables):  Ta - ta  TA - ta  TA!  (HUH!)

[Chorus Pattern]
Line 1 (7 syllables):   Tez-roq Bay-ram-Bot kel-sin (Soon may the Wellerman come)
Line 2 (8 syllables):   Is-ming-ga qo'-shiq o-lib kel-sin (To bring us sugar and tea and rum)
Line 3 (8 syllables):   Bir zum-da tay-yor bo'l-gan za-mon (One day, when the tonguin' is done)
Line 4 (6 syllables):   Bay-ram bo'-lar shu on! (HUH!) (We'll take our leave and go)
```
