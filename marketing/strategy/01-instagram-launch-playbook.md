## The verdict, before the ideas

No single video on this slate will pass 1,000,000 views on merit alone, and anyone who promises one is selling you a lottery ticket at retail. The one real path is a name-per-video reply ladder: one asset that proves a generic AI butchers Uzbek names and ours does not, then twenty to fifty cheap reply-videos answering names people put in the comments — every submission is a viewer who has already decided to DM that video to the person whose name it is, which is the exact sends-per-reach signal that moves non-follower reach, and cumulative millions across one format is a reachable target in four to eight weeks where a single-post million is not. Everything else on this slate is either the emotional proof that makes the ladder believable (the grandmother, the six shortened names), the cheap daily volume that keeps the account recommendable (the roast, the prank, the taxi series), or one dated conversion asset (Teachers' Day, 1 October). Do not waste a day on a heavy AI spectacle that showcases a capability we do not sell — we cut the 1974 TV concept for exactly that reason, and we cut every version of riding Peshta that required defeating a licensing control, because in-app availability is not a commercial sync licence and the workaround was written into the brief as a step.

### What a good result actually looks like

"Honest numbers, and the estimates are labelled as estimates. AT 24 HOURS on a zero-follower account: 800–5,000 views is the normal cold-start band and means nothing either way; 15,000+ means the test pool expanded; 40,000+ on a first-week post means the format is working. The two numbers that actually decide anything are three-second skip rate (target under 25%, 20–30% healthy, over 40% is an automatic reshoot of the first frame only — not the whole video) and sends-per-reach (published agency bands: under 0.5% = capped at followers, 0.5–1% average, 1–2% strong with Explore pickup beginning, 3%+ = large multiples of follower reach; these bands and the '3–5x a like' multiplier are agency-derived and NOT Meta-published, so treat direction as solid and magnitude as unmeasured, and calibrate against our own Insights within three weeks). AT 7 DAYS: for this account in week one, 80,000–250,000 on a single post is a genuine hit; the Socialinsider brand-account medians (4,700 for sub-30s, 10,374 for 45–60s) come from accounts that already have follower ignition, so read them as a ceiling on 'competent', not a forecast. PROBABILITY OF 1,000,000 ON A SINGLE VIDEO: low. Realistically under 5% per post even for the strongest cards here, and no source anywhere publishes a clean 'X% of videos exceed 1M' figure, so that number is my judgement and not a measurement. Across all ten assets plus their reply-ladders over six weeks at 5–7 posts a week, I would put maybe 20–35% on at least one asset crossing 1M — and that is a bet on the ladder, not on any single film. The likelier and more useful milestone, and the one to hold the team to, is 1,000,000 CUMULATIVE views across ONE format in four to eight weeks, which the name-ladder can genuinely do at 10 reply-videos a day. And the number that pays the bills is neither: it is /start commands per ?start= payload, which nobody in this dossier forecast because nobody can — measure it from post one."

## What is going viral on Instagram right now

*This is the direct answer to "identify which videos are getting viral these days" — September 2026,
Uzbek and global, as sampled on 16–17 September.*

What is actually moving on Instagram in Uzbekistan right now, September 2026, concretely. (1) Xamdam Sobirov's "Peshta" is the sound of the month — official video 90.2M views as sampled 16.09, ~2.8M/day, Shazam UZ #19, released 14–15 August, so it is roughly 4.5 weeks old and inside a 2–4 week usable window, not an evergreen. (2) The dominant creator response to it is cover and remix, not dance: Kazakh-language covers at 347K / 76K / 33K, a Russian version at 97K, an AI cover at 117K, Beatrix remix 702K, Melo Tune 385K — which means the audience for an Uzbek-pop trend is UZ+KZ+KG+RU diaspora, and anything text-dependent forfeits most of it. (3) The real dark horse is Shohruhxon & Umidaxon "Xatlar" — Shazam UZ #2, higher than Peshta, with no sub-bass for its first 19.6 seconds, which is a free 19-second slow-build bed. Also live: Sevinch Ismoilova "Avtobus" (25.5M), Gafur "Armon", AbroBey "Qimmat dunyo" (94M, the slow evergreen), Isomiddin Nur "Italianski" (three days old, the earliest-entry option). (4) The single cheapest proven local format is "Ismlar maʼnosi" — one name, its meaning, per short video — running unowned on @uzbeknames (TikTok) and @ismuz (Instagram). It is exactly our product and nobody has attached a song to it. (5) Customer-shot reaction reveals still carry the category commercially: Songfinch built $0→$250k in 30 days on customers' unpolished phone footage, at six seconds average watch time, with the un-cut stunned silence as the payload. (6) Format-wise the feed rewards a visible change in the first second, a named recipient, and a reveal at 60–70% so the loop tail replays; "wait for it", slow talking-head openers and generic "POV:" are burnt. (7) Length: on ~6M brand Reels Jan–Jun 2026, 45–60s took both engagement (0.35%) and median views (10,374) — but that is brand-account median with follower ignition, not a viral ceiling, and a cold account is decided by a small unconnected test pool reading three-second skip rate. (8) AI is not a distribution risk if labelled — correctly labelled AI content is not demoted; the only live penalty (from 31 Aug 2026) hits unlabelled AI-generated *persona* accounts, not human-run accounts using AI tools. (9) Two rules that quietly kill accounts: the anti-aggregator rule was extended to photos and carousels on 30 April 2026 (repost-built accounts stop being recommendable), and hashtags were hard-capped at 5 per post on 19 Dec 2025. (10) Mascot/character absurdity remains the cheapest repeatable 1M+ engine globally (Duolingo's "Death of Duo": 67.6M on one TikTok, ~1.7B impressions in two weeks) — relevant because we have a brand mark and no face. (11) AI-generated songs are now a first-class sound category and the winning mechanic is "many people post with this sound", not "watch my song" (Suno's "Celebrate Me" ~300,000 TikTok creates; "A Million Colors" 371,700 creates). (12) Flagged as unverified, do not treat as fact: the "AI mispronounces difficult names" genre exists as an adjacent human street-interview format but no 1M+ AI-pronunciation example was confirmed anywhere; the "a send is worth 3–5x a like" multiplier is community-derived, not Meta-published; and there is no evidence of a local-language Explore boost.

## The Peshta question, settled

Verdict: ride it in exactly one cheap slot, on our own audio, and never by defeating the picker. The song is real and spiking (90.2M, Shazam UZ #19, hook at 0:31.6, a measured 0.9-second sub-bass dropout at 0:33.1–0:34.0 and a kick slam at 0:34.04 that is the single best transition frame in Uzbek short-form this month), and its own lyrics — topping up a girl's card, buying her a Mercedes — hand a song-gift brand the counter-line "Pul solma, qoʻshiq sol" for free. But three things make it unusable the way the dossier originally proposed it. First, a Business account is limited to the Meta Sound Collection and will most likely not see Peshta in the picker at all; the widely-reported fixes (switch to Creator, or set the profile category to "Entrepreneur") are unverified workarounds, and pre-mixing the master into the file is a bare unlicensed sync that also loses the sound page, i.e. loses the only reason to ride a trend. Second, several of the proposed cuts require ducking the attached track mid-Reel (eight ducks, a 0.9s engineered mute, a 15% bed under our own song) — Instagram's picker gives you one start point and one volume, not a timeline, so those cuts force a pre-mix by construction. Third, we are in week five of a two-to-four-week window, entering as roughly the fifteenth brand. So: the only Peshta-shaped asset on this slate is card 10, and it ships on a Bayram-generated 95.8 BPM bed with our own engineered sub-bass dropout and kick return, attached as ORIGINAL AUDIO, keeping the transition mechanic, the red-and-cream art direction and the counter-line while owning an audio page other accounts can post to. Open the picker on the real account before editing anyway: if Peshta is legitimately available to this account, ship a second variant attached to it with no ducking anywhere, and post that variant TikTok-first where the sound is unrestricted. Do not switch account type to get at a track, do not pre-mix a label master, and do not boost anything carrying it — a picker is not a licence. The hedge if Peshta feels crowded: the identical cut runs against "Xatlar", whose 19.6-second bassless intro is a better bed for a slow reveal than a 0.9-second dropout.

## Before the first post — account setup

- Account type: CREATOR, not Business, and decide it before the first edit. Business accounts are limited to the Meta Sound Collection (roughly 9,000–14,000 commercially cleared tracks) and will most likely not see any current Uzbek chart track in the picker; Creator restores the full licensed catalogue, at the cost of third-party scheduling tools. A widely reported alternative is keeping Business and setting the profile category to 'Entrepreneur' — treat that as unverified and test it rather than assuming it. In-app availability is NOT a commercial licence: never boost a Reel carrying a label track, and never pre-mix a master into the file to get around a picker that is withholding it.
- Handle stays brandable and stable. The Name field — which carries roughly the same search weight as the username and is editable without breaking the handle — gets keyword-stuffed in the language people search: «Bayram | Ismli qoʻshiq · Tugʻilgan kun sovgʻasi». This is the single highest-leverage five-minute setup action on the list.
- Bio: one line of what it is, one line of who it is for, then exactly ONE link — the Telegram deep link t.me/bayram_uzbot?start=ig_bio — and a +998 phone number beside it. Uzbek gift accounts that work (e.g. a 65.8K-follower sovgʻa account with a t.me link and a +998 number in bio) all carry the phone; a bot-only bio reads as less trustworthy in this market. Kill any Linktree: one main link to the bot, not five equal links.
- Test the t.me deep link on a real iOS device and a real Android device, tapped from inside the Instagram in-app browser, before launch — a bio link may open in the in-app browser instead of jumping to Telegram, and if it does you need a redirect page that forces the app open. Use a distinct ?start= payload per surface and per asset (ig_bio, ig_gtest, ig_gulya, ig_buvim, ig_taksi, ig_janr, ig_prank, ig_roast, ig_ustoz, ig_tort, ig_pulsolma) so attribution is free and the bot can greet the user in the language of the surface they arrived from.
- Comment-to-DM automation, configured before the first post: one ASCII keyword per asset, matched case-insensitively and across variants (bayram, БАЙРАМ, bayrom), firing a single message whose body contains the raw t.me deep link. An Instagram comment does NOT open the 24-hour messaging window and the bot gets exactly one message, so a 'tap below' follow-up never arrives. Never use a keyword containing ʻ (U+02BB) — nobody types it and every miss is a permanently lost lead.
- AI labelling: no 'AI-generated profile' label is required, because that rule (live since 31 August 2026) restricts accounts built around a synthetic human persona, not human-run accounts that use AI tools. Do not front a recurring AI-generated host. If any post is ever boosted, tick the AI-generated-content box in Ads Manager from day one — undisclosed AI is reportedly the third-largest cause of ad rejection, and audiences now punish concealed AI harder than disclosed AI.
- Trial Reels require a public professional account with 1,000+ followers, so they are not available on day one. Treat the first 1,000 followers as a separate objective, then move all hook testing into Trials so failed experiments never touch the grid.
- Forbid growth automation and mass-following entirely for the first 60 days. The two things that actually get new commerce accounts limited are an aggressively pushed off-platform link from day one and repeated identical captions — so vary captions, keep the link in bio and in the auto-DM, and grow by posting.
- Alt text on every static post, under 125 characters, with the Uzbek and Russian keyword pair. It is keyword-indexed, it feeds Google Image search, it costs nothing, and nobody in this market does it.
- Set up the content log before the first post with sends-per-reach and three-second skip rate as the two tracked columns, plus start-command counts per ?start= payload pulled from the bot. Views are not a column you make decisions on.

## Do not do these

- Do not switch account type, change the profile category, or pre-mix a commercial master into the file in order to get a track the picker is withholding. The picker is withholding it because the licence does not cover this use; pre-mixing also kills the sound page, which is the only reason riding a trend is worth doing.
- Do not film a Payme button, a payment receipt, or say 'Telegramdan chiqmasdan' / 'pay inside Telegram' while the deployment's checkout provider is a stub. A stub grants a free song, so that frame is a payment that did not happen — in a market eight weeks into a documented prepaid-greeting fraud wave, that is the one failure we could not undo.
- Do not put any latency number on screen or in a caption — no '3 daqiqa', no '19 soniya'. The music render is budgeted at up to 420 seconds with a far longer queue worst case, and a title the edit has to speed-ramp to make true is a claim the first customer times with a stopwatch.
- Do not say '4 ta savol'. The wizard ships nine steps. Say 'bir necha savol' or say nothing.
- Do not claim an automated pronunciation-verification-and-re-sing guarantee. The name loop is real but bounded and degrades to delivery, so the honest and nearly-as-reassuring line is the remedy: «notoʻgʻri chiqsa — yozing, qayta yozamiz.»
- Do not claim a human wrote the song, and do not label the scenery 'AI' while leaving the song unlabelled. That is pointing at the decoy. Own the AI and sell the thing AI cannot fake — the name, pronounced right.
- Do not put a generated frame inside a documentary reaction. Across nine separate producibility reviews in this dossier, the AI insert was the frame that broke the video — it cuts the take you promised was unbroken, it announces 'ad' at the emotional peak, and it makes a viewer who clocks one synthetic frame doubt every real face in the cut.
- Do not ask Higgsfield to render a single character of Uzbek text, and do not combine a lipsync generation with a camera-move preset — they are different surfaces and the combined shot does not exist. Every prompt ends 'no text, no signage, no lettering'. Banned presets: Crash Zoom, Bullet Time, Earth Zoom, Action Figure.
- Do not use ʻ (U+02BB) in a hashtag or a comment keyword. #sovgʻa and #qoʻshiq may not resolve as tags at all, and a QOʻSHIQ trigger silently drops QO'SHIQ, QOSHIQ, КУШИК and every other way a real person will type it.
- Do not run 'drop a name in the comments and I'll make you a song' as an open thread. That is textbook contest/engagement bait, it is named in Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines, and at scale it teaches Uzbek Instagram that Bayram songs are free if your comment is funny enough. Comment-to-DM with a niche keyword is the same funnel, is policy-safe, and lands the person inside Telegram.
- Do not publish minors, non-consenting group chats, non-consenting third parties, or strangers filmed first and asked afterwards. Opt-out blurring is not consent. Every likeness release is signed before the camera rolls and covers organic posting, paid boosting and re-cuts, with a stated withdrawal route.
- Do not put a woman's name, an elder's name, or any name carrying a religious honorific under a 'difficulty', 'wrong' or 'boss fight' label. If a difficulty ladder is used at all, the label is 'AI uchun qiyin' — the machine's problem, never the person's.
- Do not make the 'GAY-rat' joke. It is a slur reading of one of the most common male names in the country, published by a brand account, in a jurisdiction where that is not recoverable in a comment thread. Gʻulomjon carries the same gʻ and none of the exposure.
- Do not label Shashmaqom as the 'wrong' genre, or put #shashmaqom on a comedy post. It is UNESCO-inscribed national heritage, in a week when national-language discourse is already live.
- Do not build the account on reposted birthday clips, scraped UGC or stitched TikToks, and never download-and-repost your own cuts between platforms. Since 30 April 2026 that is an account-level recommendability kill, not a per-post penalty.
- Do not open on a phone screen, a bot UI, a logo sting, a 'wait for it' overlay, or a slow talking head. Open on the anomaly: a face already mid-reaction, the name already landing, or the wrong pronunciation already on screen.
- Do not ship more than five hashtags, and do not design for saves — a save is a dead end for this product; a send is a lead, because the person receiving the DM is exactly the person who needs the song.
- Do not shoot two different concepts with the same grandmother, or two different concepts in a taxi. Both were duplicated across the dossier and both were resolved down to one.
